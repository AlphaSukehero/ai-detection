📐 Parameters, formulas & methodology

Relative spectral power — RSP[f1,f2] = ∫f1f2 P(f) df / ∫0.545 P(f) df, with P(f) the Welch PSD. Bands tile 0.5–45 Hz.

Spectral entropy — pk = P(fk)/ΣP, H = −Σ pk log2 pk, normalised by log2 N.

Spike density — peaks beyond 5·σMAD (σMAD = 1.4826·median|x − median x|) per second. A median-based sigma is used so a dense burst cannot raise its own threshold.

Episodes — consecutive flagged windows merge into one episode from the first window's start to the last window's end.

Pipeline — band-pass 0.5–45 Hz, 2 s windows at 50% overlap, artifact screening (EMG, flatline, saturation, gross excursion), Morlet scalograms. Uploaded images are digitised back to a signal first. Channels are averaged, so findings are localised in time, not in space.

⚠️ Research Prototype — not a medical device. Every value above is model- or signal-derived and must be reviewed by a qualified clinician before any decision. The dementia head is trained on subject-level labels; consult each model card for the split it was measured on.
